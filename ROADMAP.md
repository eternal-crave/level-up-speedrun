# 🗺️ Roadmap

Two paces, same route. The route is reordered for F2P mobile: the most F2P-critical levels come first (rewards, mechanics, level design, UI), story and cutscenes come late. Nothing is locked; this is the recommended path. Times are estimates for a full pass (read + bites + boss + a couple of quests).
