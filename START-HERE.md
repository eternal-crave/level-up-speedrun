## ⚡ 60-second quickstart

1. Open the app → pick **Speedrun (4 weeks)** or **Chill (8 weeks)**.
2. Tap **📱 Swipe 5 bites**. That's it. You've started. Seriously, that counts.
3. Tomorrow: open the app, do whatever the **Daily quests** say. Keep the 🔥.

The app always shows the **glowing node** on the Map = your next level. Never think about "what should I study"; the game decides, you just play.

## 🔋 Pick your session by battery level

ADHD energy isn't constant, so the plan isn't either. Every day, pick ONE:

| Battery | Time | Do this | Why it still counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🪫 **Low** | 5 min | One daily quest: 15 bites **or** 20 cards | Keeps the streak + spaced repetition alive. Zero days kill habits; tiny days don't. |
| 🔋 **Normal** | 30–45 min | 🃏 cards (10 min) → 📖 read one section of the current level (15 min) → ⚔️ boss fight or 🎯 side quest (10 min) | Retrieval + new input + application in one sitting |
| ⚡ **Beast mode** | 90 min | cards → read a whole level → 🧠 Recall tab + 🗣️ Feynman with the coach → ⚔️ boss → start the ⚔️ main quest → 🔬 one Play Lab | A full level cleared in a day |

**Rule:** you are never "behind." Behind is a story. Open the app and do the next tiny thing.

## 🧠 The cheat codes (learning science, already built in)

You don't need to remember these; the app does them for you. But knowing *why* makes you trust the process on boring days.

| Cheat code | One-liner | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Retrieval practice** | Pulling info *out* of your head beats putting it in 10×. Rereading feels productive and mostly isn't. | Flashcards, boss fights, pop quizzes, 🧠 Recall tab |
| **Spaced repetition** | Review right before you'd forget. Each review then lasts longer (1d → 3d → 8d → 3 weeks...). | 🃏 Cards tab schedules everything |
| **Interleaving** | Mix topics instead of blocking them. Harder in the moment, much better retention. | The feed mixes your current level with old ones; notes link levels (tap `L09`-style tags) |
| **Elaboration** | Ask "why does this work?" and "where have I seen this?" | `why` and `apply` flashcards, F2P translations everywhere |
| **Concrete examples** | Abstract rules stick to concrete games. | 🎮 Example + 📱 F2P boxes on every bite |
| **Dual coding** | Words + a picture in your head = two hooks. | 🪝 "Make it stick" images, the memory palace below |
| **Generation effect** | Making something beats reading about it. | 🎯 quests, ⚔️ main quests, the 🧱 capstone |
| **Feynman technique** | If you can't explain it simply, you don't know it yet. | 🗣️ Feynman check + 🧑‍🏫 AI coach feedback |
| **Testing effect on purpose** | Getting things *wrong* in practice makes the right answer stick harder. | Boss fights: losing a heart = learning, not failing |

## 🧯 ADHD survival kit

- **Start ugly.** The first 2 minutes are the whole battle. "Swipe 5 bites" exists so starting is basically free.
- **Timers are body doubles.** Use the ⏱ **focus sprint** (5/10/15/25 min). Phone face down. One tab. When it rings, you're allowed to stop (you usually won't).
- **Novelty rotation.** Bored of reading? Switch mode, don't quit: read → feed → cards → boss → quest → Play Lab. Every mode is progress.
- **Make it physical.** Main quests on paper are fine. Photograph and move on.
- **If-then plans.** "If I open TikTok, then I swipe 10 bites first." "If I'm on the bus, then cards."
- **Temptation bundling.** Play Labs are literally "play a mobile game." Pair the boring bits with a snack or music you love.
- **Kill the guilt loop.** Missed days? The 🧊 streak freeze has you. Missed a week? Reset the pace in Me → Settings and keep going. No shame mechanics in this game.
- **Hyperfocus is a resource, not a plan.** When it hits, ride it into a main quest or the capstone. Don't plan around it.

## 📣 Social hooks (yes, use them on yourself)

Social media hooks work because humans care what other humans see. Point that at your learning:

- **Build in public.** Post your capstone milestones. Template:
  > Day 12 of #LevelUpSpeedrun 🎮 Learned: [one idea in one sentence]. Made: [link/photo]. Tomorrow: [next thing].
  Good places: r/gamedesign, r/GameDesign Discord servers, X / LinkedIn with #gamedesign #mobilegames, a personal Notion page.
- **Find a duo.** One friend, same course or any course. Share your streak screenshot daily. Lose the streak = buy them a coffee.
- **Teach it.** Explain one bite per day to someone (or record a 30-second voice note). Teaching = the Feynman technique with an audience.
- **Collect receipts.** Your quest deliverables + capstone become your **design portfolio**. That's how F2P studios hire juniors (see DLC 2).

## 🩻 The app is a F2P game. On purpose.

Streaks, daily quests, a variable-reward chest, a saga map, hearts and a revive offer, badges, an infinite feed: every hook in the app is a real retention or monetization mechanic, pointed at learning instead of your wallet. Each one has a **Design X-Ray** button explaining what it does to you. Notice how each one feels; that's free field research for your career.

## 🏰 Memory palace: Level Up Land

A memory palace hangs ideas on places you can walk through in your head. This one is a theme park (Rogers learned level design from Disneyland, so it's on brand). The stations follow the F2P route. **How to use it:** walk it once while reading each level's TL;DR. After a week, try walking it from memory: say each station's ideas out loud. Can't recall one? That's your next review.

1. 🕹️ **Front gate · L00.** The turnstile is an arcade cabinet. You insert a coin and a cartoonist hands you a *drawing* instead of a design doc ("nobody reads text"). Banner overhead: *Designers have more fun.* → handbook of fundamentals · make docs visual · everything is gameplay, even the pause screen and the shop.
2. 🐣 **History tunnel · L01.** You walk from a lab oscilloscope → an arcade → a console → a phone. At the exit, a *Battleship* box states its goal in one sentence; a *Mouse Trap* box is on fire. A photo booth holds the whole dev team. → what a game is · one-sentence objective · new tech births new genres · hybrids · who does what.
3. 💡 **Idea fountain · L02.** The fountain spits out ridiculous ideas (a dot-eating blob, a mushroom-stomping plumber). You never throw one back. Next to it, a sculptor chips "un-fun" off a block until only fun remains. → silly ideas welcome · feed your brain weird inputs · pick an audience · the Theory of Un-Fun.
4. 📝 **Chili kitchen · L04.** A pot of chili with a ladder taped to it: one-sheet → ten-pager → beat chart → GDD. Short boil = crunch, long simmer = polish. Executives get pictures, the team gets diagrams, everything comes in threes. → the document ladder · write for your reader · the Rule of Threes · nobody reads the GDD (but you will).
5. 💎 **Money rollercoaster · F2P1.** A tiny loop (core loop) spins inside a giant loop (meta loop). The queue is a funnel, the exit sign glows **LTV > CPI**, and a faucet pours coins into a sink shaped like an upgrade. → F2P business · loops · fun in 1 min, loop in 5, hook by 15 · saw-tooth difficulty · faucets & sinks.
6. ⚡ **Power-up candy shop · L13.** Four jars: defense, offense, movement, game changers. A rubber band and an autosave clock hang under a *Love thy player* sign. A goblin hoards coins: greed is the strongest motivator, so use it kindly. → power-up families · dynamic difficulty & rubberbanding · if it feels too long, it is · rewards & currencies.
7. ⚙️ **Trap workshop · L12.** Four shelves: mechanics, hazards, props, puzzles. A boulder over your head is crossed out in red (*no rocks on the player's head*). A conductor brings in one trap at a time, like instruments. → fair, readable hazards · challenge ≠ difficulty · introduce, combine, twist · rotate mechanics like pitchers.
8. 🏰 **The castle · L09.** The castle at the end of Main Street pulls you forward: it's the *weenie*. A beat chart is nailed to its door, bread crumbs line the path, a gray clay model of the castle sits in the window, and a stamp reads *used < 3 times = cut*. → top-down design · beat chart · reuse · steer with the space · graybox · build the tutorial last.
9. 🪧 **Signpost tower · L08.** Seven signs on the tower (health, reticle, ammo, inventory, score, radar, context prompt). The center window stays clear for the action and the vital signs sit top-left. A QTE button only lights up for fireworks. → HUD classics · placement & safe area · icon rules · QTEs only for big moments.
10. 🎮 **Thumb gym · L07.** Giant stubby thumbs do push-ups on a phone. Every button fires the instant it's touched, and the dead ones answer with a "nope" buzz instead of silence. → ergonomics · meaningful mapping · press = act · no input ever does nothing.
11. 🦸 **Costume shop · L05.** The hero stands against a measuring wall: jump = ½ height, double jump = 2×, hoist = one body up. Three personality tags hang on the mannequin and its silhouette is a shadow puppet. A padlock reads *lock the 3 Cs early*. → character metrics · shape language & silhouette · personality types · companions & NPCs.
12. 🎥 **Camera crane ride · L06.** One camera rides a crane with a spotter beside it. The ride cars are camera types (static, scrolling, parallax, 2.5D, isometric, top-down, first and third person), and the turnstile says *let, lock or lend*. → one main camera · trade-offs · who controls the camera · never lose the player.
13. 🗡️ **The arena · L10.** A fighter juggles three signs: HIT, MOVE, GUARD. The scoreboard is an attack matrix, the torches burn at 60 fps, and the crowd cheers the finisher pose. → three combat verbs · ranges & attack matrix · instant timing + juice · players want to look cool · death design.
14. 👹 **Monster zoo & boss tower · L11.** Ten cages: patroller, chaser, shooter, guard, flyer, bomber, burrower, teleporter, blocker, doppelganger. Every monster winds up before it strikes. At the top, the boss dances a pattern and the player always gets the last swing. → enemy attributes · complementary behaviors · telegraphing · boss design.
15. 👥 **Food court · L14.** Players share tables (same device, network, server). The menu board lists 15 game modes, and guild banners, raid tables and housing stalls line the walls. → connect × relate · mode menu · the right player count · the MMO social toolkit F2P inherited.
16. 📡 **LiveOps stage · F2P2.** A calendar billboard flips through daily, weekly, seasonal and yearly events. A store kiosk sells the exact thing you were missing, a gacha machine shows its pity counter, a season-pass ticket reads "the right to earn", and friends hold hands so no one leaves. → monetization design · gacha & ethics · battle pass · ads · LiveOps · social glue · getting hired.
17. 📜 **Storyteller's tent · L03.** Three tent poles: characters, activities, world. Only ONE is painted weird. A salt shaker (story) sits on a steak (gameplay), and three audiences watch: story fans, lore divers and skippers. → the player's narrative · classic structure · the Triangle of Weirdness · serve all three audiences.
18. 🎬 **Empty cinema · L16.** Every seat is empty because the whole audience is crowding the SKIP button. Six projectors (FMV, animated, Flash, prerendered, puppet show, scripted event) and an 8-rung screenplay ladder. → can it be gameplay instead? · cutscene types · staging scripted events · the 8 screenplay steps.
19. 🎵 **Bandstand on mute · L15.** The band plays louder than the fight below it, but everyone's phone is on mute, so the stage flashes lights instead. The sheet music carries a "rights cleared" stamp. → plan audio early · licensing vs original · temp tracks · dynamic score & leitmotifs · design for sound-off.
20. 🏁 **Boardroom exit · L17.** An elevator with floors marked *demo > GDD > presentation > pitch* (F2P: *live metrics > prototype > deck*). A crunch clock ticks, and two cakes sit side by side: a vertical slice and a layer cake. → pitch only what matters · plan for people problems · vertical slice vs horizontal layer · sequels with the 30/70 rule.
21. 🧰 **Gift shop · BX.** The souvenirs are paperwork: a Farm Wars postcard (one-sheet), a booklet (ten-pager), enemy and boss checklists, a pitch poster. The price tags read *a TBD beats a blank*. → samples & templates · the document ladder · idea-generator lists.

## ❓ FAQ

**"This is a LOT."** The minimum viable run: TL;DR + bites + boss for each level, cards daily. That alone gets you ~80% of the book. Do quests when you have energy.

**"Do I need the book?"** No; every idea is in the notes. But it's full of Scott Rogers' drawings and jokes, and it's worth owning. Support the author.

**"Why is the order different from the book?"** The F2P route front-loads what F2P studios test you on: rewards & economy, mechanics, level design, UI. Prefer the original? Map → 📖 Book order.

**"Where's the F2P stuff the book doesn't cover?"** DLC 1 (loops, KPIs, FTUE, progression, economy, retention, teardowns) and DLC 2 (monetization, gacha, battle pass, ads, LiveOps, social, data, ethics, getting hired).
